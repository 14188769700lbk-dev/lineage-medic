## Free distribution channels

The current Show HN guidelines require something personally built, non-trivial, and directly usable. They prefer trying without signups and expect the maker to be present for discussion. LineageMedic meets the artifact requirements through its no-login replay; it does not remove the need for a truthful maker account and live participation.

Official guidelines: https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html
